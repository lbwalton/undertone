murmur

Push to talk. Text appears.

Dictation that lives in your keyboard, not in someone else's cloud.

Tap the mic key, speak, and your words land at the cursor in whatever app you are in: punctuated, cleaned up, and ready to send. Murmur runs on iPhone as a dictation-first keyboard, and on Windows and macOS as a push-to-talk tray app.

Bring your own key

Murmur connects your device directly to the transcription service you configure. The free Groq tier works out of the box, and any OpenAI-compatible endpoint is supported. There is no Murmur account and no middleman server.

Private by architecture

  • Audio goes only to the API endpoint you configured.
  • Your API key lives in the iOS Keychain.
  • The keyboard extension contains no networking and no audio code.
  • The developer collects nothing: no analytics, no tracking.
