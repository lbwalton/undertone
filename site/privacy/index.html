Murmur Voice Dictation

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-29

The short version

Murmur collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no servers operated by the developer. Your dictation audio travels directly from your device to the transcription service you configured, and the text comes straight back.

What happens when you dictate

When you record a dictation, the audio is sent over HTTPS to the API endpoint you set up in Murmur's settings (the free Groq tier by default, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint you choose). That provider transcribes it and returns text. The same applies to the optional text cleanup step, which sends the transcript, never the audio, to the chat model you configured. The developer of Murmur never sees, stores, or has access to your audio or your text. Your relationship with the transcription provider is governed by that provider's own privacy policy (for Groq, see groq.com/privacy-policy).

What stays on your device

  • Your API key, stored in the iOS Keychain (never in shared storage, never in logs).
  • Dictation history, if you leave it enabled. It has an off switch and a clear button.
  • Your personal dictionary, learned corrections, and text expansions. Expansion values are applied after formatting and never appear in any network request.
  • All settings.

The keyboard

The Murmur keyboard extension contains no networking code and no audio code, and it cannot record. It exchanges only the finished transcript and small status values with the Murmur app through Apple's App Group storage on your device. That exchange is why iOS requires the Full Access permission; nothing you type is sent anywhere by the keyboard.

The microphone

Recording only happens when you start a dictation. After a take, the microphone stays warm for a short idle window you control (including Off) so follow-up dictations are instant, and the system's orange microphone indicator is visible the entire time. When the window lapses, Murmur releases the microphone completely.

Children

Murmur collects no data from anyone, including children, and is rated 4+.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. Since Murmur's architecture has no data collection to expand quietly, meaningful changes would come with an app update you can read about in its release notes.
